Workflow guidance

Record instructions with the responsible person, due date and related work package. Critical design changes must be issued through controlled drawings and formal approvals, not chat alone.

Use the schedule, stage records and daily reports as the formal project record. Chat entries support coordination but do not replace signed instructions, RFIs or contract notices.
